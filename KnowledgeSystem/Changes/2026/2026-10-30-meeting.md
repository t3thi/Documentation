## CHG-2026-10-30-13 — Strict requirement and bounded patch scope

The accepted requested-language-only requirement remains intact. A configured
chain is still consulted by baseline Core; the unmerged patch changes the
hidden-candidate case rather than eliminating every Strict fallback. Its

backport policy remain open. The meeting does not conclusively establish a
historical introducing commit.

## CHG-2026-10-30-14 — Intentional omission versus unfinished translation

The new counterexample refines the existing requirement: work on a hidden
translation must not accidentally remove the fallback previously in use.
Separate state, language-specific suppression and Workspaces were discussed
without selecting one as the solution.

## CHG-2026-10-30-15 — Constraint on the output approach

`K-000033` must satisfy the draft-workflow case as well as explicit omission.
It remains active/tentative. `D-000003` remains proposed and prototype-scoped;
this meeting neither rejects its bounded visibility action nor adopts the
complete chain-driven output model.

## CHG-2026-10-30-16 — Composite ownership and recursive synchronization

The new requirement concerns the parent's integral one-to-many child records,
not independently reusable MM targets. In file fields the owned record is
`sys_file_reference`, not `sys_file`. The reviewed projection supplies this
scope clarification and corrects speaker attributions. Static Core inspection
qualifies the proposed behavior: relation membership/order, child-language
alignment and arbitrary child-field synchronization are separate operations.
No runtime comparison across v13/v9 or complete recursive relation audit was run.

All four changes require canonical human review. No Decision Record is accepted,
no architecture is selected and no release commitment is inferred.
