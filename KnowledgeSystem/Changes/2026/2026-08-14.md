# Semantic clarifications proposed on 2026-08-14

## CHG-2026-08-14-01

target: K-000017
operation: refine

### Before

Language-identity persistence, API representation and migration of local
numeric IDs and locales required wider decisions, while the dependencies on
the non-language meanings of `-1` and `0` remained implicit.

### After

A semantic language identity can fully replace the current
`sys_language_uid` contract only after `-1` Language-All behavior, the `0`
Site-default role and the `0` structural-lead role have explicit contracts
outside Language Identity.

### Evidence

- `current-state:3612574c0a4db050276c0e2602c2d812b7a87c8d`
- `transcript:2026-07-31`
- `transcript:2026-06-11`
- `transcript:2026-06-26`

### Reason

The accepted responsibility separation already established each concern, but
the migration dependency between them was not stated explicitly.

## CHG-2026-08-14-02

target: K-000008
operation: refine

### Before

Existing-target reconciliation, overwrite authority, provenance, detachment
and deactivation were recorded as broad lifecycle questions.

### After

translations, target editability and visual ownership, permitted local changes,
and the fate and retained provenance of detached or deactivated synchronized
records.

- `transcript:2026-06-11`

without selecting answers or an API.

## CHG-2026-08-14-03

target: K-000019
operation: refine

### Before

Structural ownership, representation and migration were open, while the
relation to the Default Language's current structural lead was described mainly
inside the candidate-architecture comparison.

### After

Structural Identity must explicitly replace the structural reference and lead
currently coupled to `sys_language_uid = 0` before that value can lose its
non-language role; all candidate structure models remain undecided.

### Evidence

- `current-state:3612574c0a4db050276c0e2602c2d812b7a87c8d`
- `transcript:2026-06-26`

### Reason

The dependency clarifies why structure is part of language-identity migration
without promoting the preferred hidden layer into a decision.
