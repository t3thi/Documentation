# Architecture and knowledge bootstrap, 2026-08-11

## CHG-2026-08-11-01

target: D-000001
operation: implement

### Before

Current initiative understanding was maintained primarily in the narrative
Current State and historical Meeting Minutes. Durable propositions, decision
authority and semantic transitions did not have stable canonical records.

### After

D-000001 implements a layered Git-native system of retained transcript evidence,
reviewed Meeting Minutes, Semantic Change Logs, Knowledge Objects, separate
Decision Records, Topic Syntheses and deterministic publication views. The
Current State baseline at revision
`3612574c0a4db050276c0e2602c2d812b7a87c8d` seeded K-000001 through K-000027.

The bootstrap is a representation migration. It does not turn preferences into
Core decisions or make the Current State independent corroboration of its own
underlying meeting evidence.

### Evidence

- `policy:knowledge-system`
- `policy:knowledge-handbook`
- `current-state:3612574c0a4db050276c0e2602c2d812b7a87c8d`

### Reason

The explicit architecture mandate required a Git-reconstructable,
provenance-aware semantic model while preserving historical evidence and the
narrative quality of the Current State.
