# Output policy requires wider decisions

## Statement

The product semantics of missing, disabled and intentionally absent variants
are not decided. Open questions include whether normal fallback offers an
optional terminal default, where per-position intent is configured and how Core
rendering, Extbase and custom queries evaluate it consistently.

## Rationale

These choices affect existing site output and cannot be inferred from
structural relation alone.

## Consequences

Any proposal requires product semantics and compatibility analysis; strict
single-language output remains a separate active requirement.
