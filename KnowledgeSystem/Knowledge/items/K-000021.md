# Compatibility and ownership require wider decisions

## Statement

Cross-cutting decisions remain open about acceptable persisted-data versus
runtime complexity, measurable operational cost, compatibility and deprecation,
extension APIs, migration and the responsible Core and product owners.

## Rationale

The initiative can collect evidence, implement bounded fixes and formulate
recommendations, but does not claim sole authority over TYPO3 strategy or Core
architecture.

## Consequences

Proposals must identify decision authority and quantify record volume, write
amplification, queries, Reference Index, Workspaces and backend usability where
relevant.
