# Field synchronization has two control models

## Statement

`l10n_mode=exclude` enforces synchronization through TCA configuration.
`config.behaviour.allowLanguageSynchronization=true` makes a field eligible
for an editor choice stored in `l10n_state`. Core processes both in the same
DataMapProcessor pipeline, but selects their fields through different scopes.

## Rationale

The distinction captures who controls the state. A shared execution path does
not make configuration-enforced and editor-selectable synchronization
equivalent.

## Consequences

Any consolidation must preserve the enforced no-opt-out behavior separately
from editable `parent`, `source` and `custom` choices, with migration and
extension-compatibility rules.
