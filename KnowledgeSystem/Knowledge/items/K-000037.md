# Parent-owned inline children are aligned to the stored parent language

## Statement

TYPO3 v15 and 14.3 now align language-aware, parent-owned `inline` and `file`
children to the language actually stored for their parent, after relations
have been remapped. Triggers are parent creation, a changed parent language,
or newly submitted inline children. Existing siblings are included; nested
children follow their aligned parent. A submitted differing child language
is not protected by the earlier proposal's explicit-value exception.

## Rationale

Gerrit 87595 merged on 2026-10-17 and 96783 on 2026-10-18. The official
abandonment of 95476 names both. Static inspection confirms that alignment
uses fixed-field writes, writes workspace versions in a workspace and clears
translation pointers when aligning to `0` or Language All. Per-child-language
tracking bounds cyclic input.

## Consequences

This is not a universal ban on mixed-language relations: MM relations are
excluded, direct child-language edits are not covered, and inconsistent old
records are not globally repaired. They are aligned when a relevant parent
operation occurs. Alignment writes do not separately check child-table,
page or language-field access, as documented by the merged change. This
contract requires explicit review because it differs from 95476's narrower
proposal. It does not adopt the initiative's future structural architecture.
