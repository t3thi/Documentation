# Shared hidden structure is a preferred hypothesis

## Statement

The current preference for investigation is a contentless, language-neutral
structure layer whose shared positions connect real output-language records.
The layer should remain invisible in normal editorial and frontend output.

## Rationale

This direction separates visible default-language content from structural lead
and avoids duplicating a placeholder into every real language.

## Consequences

It is not selected Core architecture. Its exact entity, local ordering and
absence semantics, permissions, queries, APIs, Workspaces, references and
migration require prototyping and lifecycle design.
