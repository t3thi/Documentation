# Language identity requires wider decisions

## Statement

The authoritative language identity and its database and API representation are
not selected. A semantic identity can fully replace today's
`sys_language_uid` contract only after its non-language meanings are modelled
separately: `-1` as record-wide Language-All synchronization intent, and `0`
as both a Site's default-language role and today's structural lead. The future
identity value may identify only a real human language or variant. Mapping
ambiguous Site Language IDs and locales, migration, and the visibility of any
retained internal numeric key require decisions beyond the initiative alone.

## Rationale

The initiative can establish the identity problem and recommend a direction,
but BCP 47 addresses only language identity. It cannot by itself replace
Language-All behavior or decide what assumes structural leadership when the
Default Language is no longer privileged. The initiative cannot unilaterally
define those Core-wide persistence, lifecycle and compatibility contracts.

## Consequences

BCP 47 remains a preference, including the open choice between storing a tag
and mapping it to an internal identifier. A complete migration must also define
an explicit Site-default rule, a synchronization lifecycle for the former
`-1` behavior and a Structural-Identity model that replaces the structural
role coupled to `0`. None of those replacement models is selected by this
dependency.
