# Synchronization contract requires wider decisions

## Statement

Wider decisions are required for the record-wide target and full-parity
contract, any later multi-select scope, the enforced field state and possible
replacement of `l10n_mode=exclude`, and every activation and detachment
transition.

## Rationale

The initiative's direction and reuse analysis do not establish a complete API,
lifecycle or migration policy.

## Consequences

Names such as `language_sync`, `ctrl.languageSyncField` and
`enforceLanguageSynchronization` remain proposals, not adopted configuration.
