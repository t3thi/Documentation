# Intentional absence needs explicit output semantics

## Statement

A missing variant can mean not translated yet, intentionally absent,
structurally unnecessary or available through fallback. Where editors need an
intentional omission, output policy must represent whether fallback continues or
stops for that position.

## Rationale

The same missing or disabled record currently can lead to fallback even though
editor intent differs.

## Consequences

The representation and configuration scope remain open and require backward
compatibility across Core rendering, Extbase and custom queries.
