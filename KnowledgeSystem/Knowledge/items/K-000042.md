# Prevent invalid translation links without deleting translations

## Statement

The preferred direction is to validate changes to an original's
effective language in `DataHandler`, across moves and direct edits, so
existing translations do not acquire an invalid parent. Do not delete
translations as an implicit consequence of a move.

## Rationale

A move-specific guard cannot cover a language change in the editing form.
Gerrit 93063 diagnoses missing, non-default and Language-All parents.
Benni proposed blocking the `0` to `-1` transition when translations
exist, displaying orphans in Layout and allowing repair in FormEngine.
Preventing one transition neither repairs old data nor covers all moves.

## Consequences

The central validation and recovery path are proposals, not implemented
behavior. Verify which orphans existing Layout and FormEngine paths can
display and edit, and test relevant writes in Live and Workspaces. The
meeting did not abandon 93063 or choose a replacement for 93819.
