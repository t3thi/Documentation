# Overlapping Free and Mixed comparison changes were superseded

## Statement

The narrow Free-Mode rendering change 92585 was abandoned on 2026-08-07 with
95170 named as its further-developed successor. The overlapping Free- and
Mixed-Mode comparison change 94917 remained formally open after 95170 and its
14.3 backport 95199 merged on 2026-08-10, then was abandoned on 2026-08-27.

## Rationale

The successor corrects comparison views containing Connected- and Free-Mode
languages, including a missing Default-Language item case.

## Consequences

Both historical changes remain addressable as superseded work. The merged
replacement is the bounded interim correction and does not select the future
editing or structural model.
