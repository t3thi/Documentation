# Support mostly connected, selectively different structures

## Statement

A language variant should retain the benefits of a shared structural majority
while adding, omitting, replacing or reordering selected positions. A small
local exception must not force the editor to fabricate hidden default-language
content or abandon every useful connection.

## Rationale

Real project and editor cases occupy the middle ground between identical
translations and fully independent structures.

## Consequences

The product contract must preserve independent outcomes while Core maintains
relation integrity. Removing today's mode controls depends on meeting that
contract first.
