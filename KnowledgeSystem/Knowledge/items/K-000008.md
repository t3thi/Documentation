# Synchronization lifecycle remains unresolved

## Statement

The lifecycle for explicit record-wide synchronization is not designed. Open
questions include target scope; adoption, replacement or reconciliation of
existing translations; editability and visual identification of synchronized
targets; overwrite authority; generated-record provenance; later languages;
and the behavior of detachment, disablement, deletion, restoration, relations,
permissions, versions and Workspaces. If a generated target is detached, its
subsequent independence and retained origin and synchronization history also
require an explicit contract.

## Rationale

Creating concrete records turns one read-time special value into write-time and
lifecycle obligations. Activating synchronization over existing records,
allowing local edits or later detaching generated records can destroy
intentional differences or obscure ownership if those transitions are
implicit.

## Consequences

The synchronization direction cannot be called production-ready, and target
multi-select or field exceptions must not precede a defined, idempotent base
lifecycle.
