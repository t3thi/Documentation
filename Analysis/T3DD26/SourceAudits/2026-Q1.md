## Scope and interpretation rules

This dossier covers every Markdown file under `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/01`, `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/02`, and `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/03`, plus the complete transcripts dated 2026-02-13, 2026-02-20, 2026-03-13, 2026-03-20, and 2026-03-27.

All statuses use the requested controlled vocabulary:

- **Current Core Behavior**
- **Problem**
- **Idea**
- **Discussed Direction**
- **Preferred Direction**
- **Open Question**
- **Planned**
- **In Progress**
- **Implemented**
- **Analytically Derived Recommendation**

Patch states are the state evidenced on the meeting date, not a claim about their present status. Transcript statements are treated as informal meeting evidence, not automatically as team consensus. Where speaker labels or automatic transcription are doubtful, the dossier attributes cautiously or describes the exchange without asserting a named speaker.

## Q1 bottom line for the T3DD26 narrative

1. **The most defensible Q1 story is incremental, not a settled replacement architecture.** The initiative moved from inventorying `sys_language_uid = -1` occurrences toward validating findings, locating missing tests, and creating small mergeable test patches before functional change. This is a **Preferred Direction**, supported by 2026-03-13 and therefore later and more specific than the comparatively linear phase plan recorded on 2026-01-09. Sources: `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/01/09.md:69-77`; `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/03/13.md:41-51`; `Transcripts/2026-03-13 12-00-26 - Meeting der Initiative.txt:516-577,689-742`.

2. **Q1 contains strong evidence that the special values `-1` and `0` are entangled in semantic checks, but not a decided method for removing either.** A literal constant was discussed as an incremental aid; semantic helpers were considered necessary for implicit comparisons; Core-maintainer input was still required before choosing the removal concept. Status: **Discussed Direction** plus **Open Question**. Sources: `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/03/13.md:41-47`; `Transcripts/2026-03-13 12-00-26 - Meeting der Initiative.txt:440-490,516-542`.

3. **Several concrete changes already hardened today's model.** The target-site-language copy restriction was merged; Free Mode copy filtering, Free Mode display, orphan warnings, dbdoctor detection, and Workspace `Language All` test coverage were at different unfinished stages. These are evidence of a safe evolutionary path, not implementation of a new language model. Sources: `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/02/13.md:17-41`; `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/03/13.md:23-31`; `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/03/20.md:23-45`; `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/03/27.md:42-46`.

4. **The Q1 architecture discussion that most directly supports the proposed session trade-off is narrower than “complete language layers.”** In the IRRE-child debate, deliberate duplication/synchronization was defended because it can simplify lookup/output logic, while Core reviewers raised database-economy concerns. The initiative explicitly said the general acceptability of database synchronization must be settled with Core maintainers. Status: **Open Question**. Sources: `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/03/27.md:32-40`; `Transcripts/2026-03-27 12-02-56 - Meeting der Initiative.txt:371-420,582-664`.

5. **Q1 alone does not evidence BCP 47, Editing Language, Shadow Records, a neutral identity layer, complete language layers, an explicit “all languages” boolean, or `behaviour.enforceLanguageSynchronisation`.** The January record mentions future string-based language identifiers, but no BCP-47 tag model is named. Those concepts must be sourced from other periods or presented only as externally supplied hypotheses. Source: `MeetingMinutes/Weekly/2026/01/16.md:57-69`.
