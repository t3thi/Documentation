## Topic 3: Strict Fallback and Editorial Compatibility

The team revisited [patch #94510](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/94510). It stops fallback under `fallbackType: strict` when a translation is hidden, but retains the configured chain when a translation is missing. Eric was willing to retain that existing chain behavior in TYPO3 14.3 because some projects rely on it to fall back to another translation without displaying the default-language record. The team discussed taking the smaller fix separately from Benni's broader TYPO3 v15 proposal, but left the release and backport decision open.

The same `hidden` flag can express intentional absence or identify a translation still being edited. Stopping fallback immediately supports the former but can remove live content during the latter. The participants prepared a request for an Important changelog entry explaining the changed behavior and suggesting a configured Workspace as a way to continue editing without changing live output. [Martin posted the review](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/94510/comment/dcebd4a2_8f21bbdd/) during the meeting, asking whether the change would be breaking and whether it also affects translations restricted by `starttime`, `endtime` or `fe_group`. The participants consulted the [TCA `ctrl` reference](https://docs.typo3.org/m/typo3/reference-tca/main/en-us/Ctrl/) but did not settle the behavior of those other restrictions.

## Topic 4: Language Mode and the Position of Language Zero

The participants preferred Benni's [`languageMode` proposal in #97535](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/97535) over the alternative [`fallbackType: connected` in #97455](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/97455). The proposed modes `overlays` and `free` avoid reusing Connected Mode, which already describes how records are related in the backend. The team expected overlay rendering to include floating records: an independent record created in the requested language should still render. This was a preference between proposals, not a Core adoption decision.

Eric reported that, in his tests of #97535, omitting `0` from the chain avoided falling back to default-language content. He initially argued that the chain should be followed in order regardless of where `0` appeared. André raised the case of a hidden default-language record: if it is filtered out before overlays are evaluated, a translation after `0` might not be reachable at all. His [Slack example](https://typo3.slack.com/archives/C05D7UF1L8M/p1794570727859619) used requested language `5` with fallbacks `1,0,2`. Eric held back a Gerrit comment and planned to tabulate observed results with `0` first, in the middle and last. Trimming the entries after `0` on save was discussed only if testing confirms that they cannot take effect.

## Topic 5: Preventing and Repairing Orphaned Translations

[Patch #93063](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/93063) distinguishes three reasons why a translation has no valid parent in the Layout module: the referenced record is missing, belongs to a non-default language, or is set to Language All. In his [review](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/93063/comment/4886b314_eb1fba73/), Benni proposed a separate `DataHandler` guard against changing `sys_language_uid` from `0` to `-1` when translations exist, as reported in [Forge ticket #108358](https://forge.typo3.org/issues/108358). He also proposed a dedicated orphan state in Layout and a FormEngine path for unlinking or reassigning existing orphans.

The team favored preventing new invalid relationships over improving warning labels alone. They discussed whether the existing warning and editing paths already cover some recovery cases, and whether to replace or retain the diagnostic patch after checking those paths. They did not decide to abandon the change or assume that a guard for one transition would repair existing data.

## Topic 6: Language Changes When Moving Connected Content

The discussion of [patch #93819](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/93819) distinguished the move gesture from the underlying change to an original record's language. Benni's [review](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/93819/comment/64039f23_c2c9e2ec/) reported that moving or pasting an original into a different language can leave translations pointing to an invalid parent, including a parent in the same language as its translation. Changing the language directly in the editing form can cause the same problem, outside the patch's Free-Mode move guard.

The team discussed blocking invalid language changes in `DataHandler` across these entry points. Deleting translations as a side effect of a move was considered too destructive; a confirmation remembered from another change was not part of the patch under discussion. The group did not agree on a replacement implementation.

## Topic 7: Copy Targets, Storage Folders, and PID Zero

The final discussion concerned [patch #92777](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/92777). In his [review](https://review.typo3.org/c/Packages/TYPO3.CMS/+/92777/comment/f3cc52ba_f54ffc98/), Benni asked whether requiring a target page translation should also restrict copies into storage folders or to `pid=0`, particularly in TYPO3 14.3. Eric reiterated the initiative's use of both configured Site Languages and the target page's available languages for ordinary content. André pointed out that shared translated records, such as categories, can live at `pid=0` without a Site Language context. The discussion also considered folders outside any site and whether they should be exempt from a page-translation requirement.

The rules for storage folders and `pid=0`, and whether the change is suitable for a maintenance release, remained open. Eric invited the participants to continue reviewing the remaining changes before the next discussion.
